AI-Based Early Warning System

For Hospital Resource Shortage

Project Motive & Objective

Hospitals frequently face unexpected crises due to the sudden depletion of life-saving resources like Oxygen, ICU beds, and critical blood groups.

This system is designed to monitor real-time hospital inventory. By utilizing demand forecasting and threshold logic, it provides Early Red Alerts before a resource completely runs out, ensuring uninterrupted medical care and saving patient lives.
